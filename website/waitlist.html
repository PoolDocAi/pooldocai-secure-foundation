Product Questions & Safety

Frequently Asked Questions

Open a question to learn what is in the demo today, what is planned for the pilot, and how safety and source transparency work.

What does “15-minute setup” include?

The 15-minute call is intended for a short orientation and initial guidance on the selected pilot path—for example, discussing where a website button could go or how a team would try the demo. It does not promise a fully configured knowledge base, migration of every manual, technician account provisioning, a completed production integration, or a live dispatch workflow within 15 minutes. Company-specific procedures, website changes, account setup, and testing may require preparation or follow-up after the call.

Does route export mean PoolDocAI dispatches technicians or integrates with my route software?

No—not in this public demo. The demo can preview a Pre-Job Summary for review. Exportable summaries and full multi-crew dispatch/route export are separate capabilities; full dispatch and route-software integration are planned production capabilities, not currently live. Any pilot scope will be confirmed before enrollment.

What does PoolDocAI do?

PoolDocAI guides homeowners through initial troubleshooting triage, captures the symptoms and checks they have already completed, and lets a technician pick up where the homeowner left off, with the issue and completed checks visible in the Pre-Job Summary. It also helps identify likely next checks, supports water-balancing calculations, and prepares a clear Pre-Job Summary for the service team.

What knowledge base does PoolDocAI use?

PoolDocAI is designed to guide troubleshooting using a curated pool-equipment reference library and relevant manufacturer or technical resources when available. It helps narrow down likely causes and next checks, but recommendations should always be verified against the documentation for your exact equipment model.

Can we add our own company procedures and train it to follow our guidelines?

Yes. During onboarding and the early-access pilot, participating companies can work with PoolDocAI to incorporate company-specific manuals, service procedures, preferred workflows, and escalation rules. The goal is to tailor troubleshooting to your team’s procedures and safety requirements.

Can PoolDocAI identify equipment from a photo?

Photo identification is available only in the authenticated pilot workflow. Join the waitlist to request access. Suggested make/model details must always be checked against the equipment label or manufacturer documentation.

Can I text troubleshooting links to customers if my business has no website?

The Complete Track includes the website chat experience. Customer-specific textable troubleshooting links are not active in this public demo and are planned for a future pilot phase. The Tech-Only Track focuses on technician troubleshooting.

What happens if a user reports a serious safety hazard?

Reports of gas odor, smoke, electrical shock, chemical fumes, or other serious hazards should be treated as a stop-work situation. Move to a safe location and contact emergency services or the appropriate utility when there is immediate danger. PoolDocAI is not a substitute for emergency responders, manufacturer instructions, or a qualified professional.

How does PoolDocAI handle routine maintenance precautions?

Some checks may require you to acknowledge a caution before continuing. An acknowledgement does not make a task safe for every person or equipment model. Follow the manufacturer’s procedure, and do not proceed if a step involves gas, electricity, pressurized filters, or another hazard you are not qualified to handle.

How does PoolDocAI show where an answer comes from?

When available, PoolDocAI can show the name or link of a reference associated with its guidance. Open the reference and confirm that it applies to your exact equipment model; AI summaries and source descriptions may be incomplete.

What if I only want a Field Technician app?

The Tech-Only Track is designed for businesses that want technician-focused troubleshooting without homeowner chat. Plans are Tech Starter ($49/mo), Tech Solo / Route ($79/mo), Tech Pro Team ($149/mo), and Tech Fleet ($269/mo). Ask about early-access availability and onboarding for your team.

Are homeowner sessions and technician sessions separate?

Yes. Complete plans are designed with separate monthly allowances for homeowner sessions and technician sessions, so customer troubleshooting does not use up your technicians’ session allowance.

How does the planned +25 bonus-session offer work?

The proposed pilot offer is coming soon: it may provide 25 bonus technician sessions after 15 verified job close-outs in a billing cycle. This is not active in the public demo and would be subject to the pilot’s close-out verification rules.

How are waitlist and demo details used?

Information you submit is used to review your waitlist or demo request, follow up about early access, and generate troubleshooting responses. PoolDocAI does not sell waitlist contact details. See the for more detail.
