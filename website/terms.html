Terms of Use

Draft — formal legal review required. This page is a working product draft, not a representation that PoolDocAI’s terms satisfy every applicable legal requirement. Do not activate paid commercial access until the terms, privacy disclosures, business identity, contact details, and applicable consumer/business obligations have been reviewed and approved.

These draft terms describe the intended use of PoolDocAI, an AI-assisted pool-equipment troubleshooting and service-support product. In these terms, “PoolDocAI,” “we,” and “our” refer to the PoolDocAI service operator; “you” refers to the person or organization using the service.

1. Service and account access

PoolDocAI may provide conversational troubleshooting, manufacturer-source research, document search, technician workflows, service-request summaries, usage reporting, and company knowledge-management features. Available features depend on account, plan, configuration, and release status. You are responsible for protecting login credentials, promptly revoking access for departing users, and ensuring users are authorized to access your company’s data.

2. AI-assisted information is not a guarantee

Responses may be incomplete, inaccurate, outdated, or inappropriate for a particular installation. Sources and model outputs do not replace the equipment manufacturer’s instructions, local codes, licensing requirements, chemical labels, inspection, or professional judgment. Confirm important information against current authoritative documentation and the actual equipment before acting.

3. Safety responsibilities

Do not use PoolDocAI to override a safety hold, bypass a protective device, or perform work beyond your training or authorization. Electrical, gas, chemical, pressure-vessel, entrapment, structural, and other hazardous conditions require appropriate isolation and qualified professional judgment. Stop work and keep people clear when conditions appear unsafe. AI output is not an emergency-response service and does not dispatch a technician unless an explicitly contracted workflow states otherwise.

4. Company documents and permission to process

When you upload a manual, service record, webpage, image, SOP, policy, or other material, you represent that you have authority to submit it for the selected processing and reference purposes. You must not upload material unlawfully obtained, confidential third-party content without authorization, or content that you are not permitted to provide. Company-specific manuals, SOPs, safety policies, and private operating procedures remain private company references by default. Optional contribution to a general knowledge base requires separate rights, an affirmative opt-in, and PoolDocAI platform-owner review; submission does not automatically publish content or train a model.

5. Acceptable use

You may not use the service to violate laws, infringe intellectual-property or privacy rights, probe another tenant’s records, bypass access controls, upload malicious content, disrupt the service, or misrepresent AI-generated guidance as a verified inspection or repair. You may not attempt to use one company’s access to retrieve another company’s manuals, service requests, users, or diagnostic history.

6. Data, privacy, and retention

Use of the service is also subject to the Privacy Policy draft. Troubleshooting content, usage events, uploaded material, account information, and service-request details may be processed to provide and secure the service. Do not include unnecessary sensitive or personal information in troubleshooting messages. Retention, deletion, and customer-specific processing commitments must be confirmed in the final approved policy and applicable contract.

7. Availability and changes

Features may be changed, suspended, or retired while the service is being developed. Public demonstrations have limited functionality and are not equivalent to a production subscription. Except where a final contract states otherwise, the service is provided on an “as available” basis; any final warranty, liability, indemnity, and service-level provisions remain subject to legal review.

8. Plans and billing

Plan names and proposed entitlements may be displayed for evaluation. Paid checkout, subscription changes, refunds, taxes, and commercial billing are not enabled by this draft. Do not treat a displayed plan or price as an executed subscription agreement until the final pricing and billing terms are published and the authorized checkout is active.

9. Contact and updates

A dedicated legal-notice and privacy-contact address has not yet been published. Until it is, use the Early Access page only for ordinary product inquiries and do not send highly sensitive material through an open form. The production terms should identify the correct legal entity, physical or mailing address where required, contact channels, governing law, dispute process, effective date, and a versioned change-notice process before commercial launch.
