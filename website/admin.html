Company SOPs and manuals

Upload manuals, SOPs, images, PDFs, Word documents, or add a public webpage URL. PoolDocAI extracts text, runs OCR on supported scans/images, chunks it for retrieval, and distills opted-in lessons into a pending review queue. Company-private reference access is independent of shared-learning consent.

PDFs with scanned pages and image files use Gemini OCR. HEIC images are converted before OCR. Password-protected files and pages behind a login cannot be imported.

Ready documents are private-searchable for this company. Shared knowledge still requires PoolDocAI platform-owner approval.

Plan and billing

Stripe checkout is prepared but remains unavailable until the platform owner configures a Stripe account, recurring Price IDs, and a verified webhook. Plan metadata alone never activates paid features.

Usage for current billing period

AI costs are estimates only and remain unpriced until current provider rate cards are configured. Storage shows current manual-library bytes; request and document processing metrics use the active Stripe billing period when available; otherwise the dashboard falls back to the UTC calendar month.
