1. Three Original Logo Concepts (Owner Decision Memo)

Awaiting Owner Concept Selection (L1, L2, or L3)
Concept L1 PD Precision Monogram

Utilitarian Engineering Precision

Geometric interlocking P and D forming an engineered caliper and diagnostic bracket. Clean 45° chamfers, ultra-crisp at 16px/24px favicon sizes. Zero clip art.

Strengths:
  • Maximum legibility down to 16px favicon.
  • Reinforces professional diagnostic copilot identity.
  • High contrast in both bright sunlight and dark terminals.
Concept L2 PD + Fluid Precision Flow

Hydrodynamic Circulation Vector

Technical PD monogram grounded by a disciplined hydraulic flow curve representing pool pump circulation, filter return, and fluid engineering.

Strengths:
  • Immediate visual connection to pool water circulation.
  • Balanced blend of software tech and field plumbing reality.
  • Distinguishable in multi-tenant widgets and app stores.
Concept L3 Diagnostic Gauge & Telemetry

Equipment Telemetry & Calibration

Calibrated 270° pressure gauge arc with precision indicator needle and central PD telemetry hub. Bridges filter pressure, motor RPM, and water balance.

Strengths:
  • Instantly communicates measurement, accuracy, and testing.
  • Appeals directly to service technicians managing filter PSI.
  • Eye-catching amber needle adds diagnostic urgency.
