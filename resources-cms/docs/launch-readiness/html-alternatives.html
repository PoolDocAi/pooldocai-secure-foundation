17 — Diagnostic sequence

Safety screening and documentation continue throughout. Immediate hazards take priority. The sequence does not require completing unsafe work or work outside the technician's qualifications.

  1. Capture the reported problem.
  2. Identify the exact equipment.
  3. Find the applicable manufacturer documentation.
  4. Screen for hazards.
  5. Perform appropriate tests within qualifications.
  6. Record eliminated causes and evidence.
  7. Distinguish confirmed findings from suspected causes.
  8. Repair, escalate or identify the next action.
  9. Verify operation and record limitations.
  10. Document the outcome and assign follow-up.

This semantic HTML sequence is proposed instead of the unavailable diagram. It introduces no equipment illustration or external image.
