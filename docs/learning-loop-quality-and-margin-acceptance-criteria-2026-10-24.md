**Owner policy:** Confirmed 2026-10-24. **Status:** implementation design and QA instructions for PR #14 only. This document is NOT authority to change production, live database, Stripe, paid infrastructure or customer subscriptions.

## 1. Immutable commercial rule unless the owner separately decides otherwise
PoolDocAI's **approved public subscription prices remain in force** as its real cost per diagnostic case decreases. A rising share of correctly answered, verified knowledge-base cases should raise **gross/contribution margin**, not reduce prices, grant free seats, expand monthly pooled session allowances or trigger founders discounts. Any change requires an explicit owner commercial decision, NOT an automated efficiency, billing, entitlement or AI rule. See `docs/approved-pricing-and-entitlements-2026-10-24.md` for the authoritative eight tiers and paid add-ons. Preserve legacy subscriptions.

Pricing remains independent of the diagnostic route: a company consumes **one new monthly pooled diagnostic case** whether answered using approved internal reference, a cheap model, a live official manufacturer source or an approved reviewed repair. The same case's follow-up turns stay tied to that case, with separately bounded API consumption. Do not double-bill for a retry. Do not promise unlimited messages or zero marginal cost.

- **P1 company SOP discovery:** `companyManualRows` built from `search_company_manual_chunks_hybrid` currently maps document title/page/chunk/content, but **does not populate explicit source brand/model/symptom/revision fields**. Add durable structured metadata and link it to specific chunks/doc revisions; do not guess model identity from plain narrative text.
- **P0 authority citation check:** `internalProcedureIsUsable` currently depends on model-supplied `structuredSourceLabel === "internal_manual"` plus presence of *some* `procedureTextPresent`; require a server-verified selected evidence ID and applicable procedural text, not merely a self-described source label + unrelated text. Distinguish globally approved lesson, company-private manual/SOP, official manufacturer PDF/page and verified web source in UI and audit. - **P0 unverified learning pollution:** `capture_learning_case` may queue an authenticated chatbot answer for shared review when company/case consents are true, while setting `technician_verified:false`. This is a **hypothesis/observed dialog**, NOT a completed repair. Only validated resolved outcomes may be published as "verified fix" knowledge. Demo/customer-reported "solved" can be a candidate but carries lower provenance and requires authoritative manual corroboration + platform review before reuse. - **Field notes nuance:** New `save_technician_job` code now marks an outcome `resolved` plus nonempty written resolution/repair result as `technician_verified` and queues only such cases. ## 3. Required architecture and classification
### A. Event capture and explicit outcomes
Persist tenant-scoped case context using minimization: equipment category, manufacturer, **verified** family/model/firmware/revision where known, explicit symptom, observed fault/code, tests attempted, results, source IDs, final technician-reported resolution, optional qualified-review status, timestamps and case ID. Avoid raw customer contact data in reusable knowledge. Never equate "AI suggested", "user tapped step completed", "notes saved", "customer says fixed", and "verified repaired".

Use at least these outcome trust levels:
1. `unverified_observation` — conversation/technician note, still in progress; company-private.
2. `customer_reported_resolution` — a user reports improvement; candidate, not verified repair.
3. `technician_reported_resolution` — named authenticated technician records actual successful check/repair; candidate, not yet platform-approved general reference.
4. `platform_verified_lesson` — reviewers corroborate against model-applicable OEM data or appropriate evidence and separately verify privacy/consent, scope and safety; eligible for cross-company knowledge-first routing.
5. `rejected|superseded|consent_withdrawn` — excluded immediately from shared retrieval and caches.

Use a normalized, versioned lesson document with category/symptom/error-code applicability, equipment make/model and revision compatibility, evidence references, source type, source page/document version, resolution result, confidence/review history, safety class, private/shared scope, revocation/expiry flags, duplicate/supersession mapping, and timestamps. Deduplicate recurring cases without suppressing novel fault patterns or evidence of different root causes. Determine the field-level format with migrations reviewed for RLS, consent and performance; no destructive migrations.

### B. Consent, access and reviewer quality
- Company-private SOPs, policies, customer records, and manual extracts **stay company private** by default. Do NOT merge tenant-specific procedures into general platform lessons merely because technician notes were captured.
- Shared diagnostic learning requires the governing company opt-in **and** applicable case-level/document rights/terms, plus owner-controlled review; true permission must be independently checked again transactionally at publish time. Model/browser claims do not authorize cross-tenant sharing.
- Platform reviewer sees evidence provenance and flags: resolved but no source, conflicting result, high-risk gas/electric/pressure/chemical, model ambiguity, revision mismatch, unverified code, suspected personally identifying content, wrong category. Require qualified escalation and authoritative references for hazardous and chemistry-dosing content.
- Withdrawal/rejection/supersession invalidates index/search eligibility and cached answer paths promptly, including derived lessons and embeddings as applicable. Keep company-private job record unless otherwise requested; audit approval/revocation without customer-identifying text.
- A mere 80-character text threshold or cosine similarity score cannot substitute for evidence.

### C. Retrieval + action routing
**Desired pipeline:**
1. Resolve tenant and mode on the trusted server. Parse case state without re-asking answered questions. Use conservative hazard classification before any external or internal instructions.
2. First retrieve **tenant-private** documents/lessons for that tenant and approved platform knowledge in separate scoped queries. Filter model/source-compatibility and symptom/error-code applicability with deterministic safety metadata and retrieval score. A generic company manual page or a different model may assist identification, never a repair without corroboration.
3. Select a narrow, server-verified evidence set with source ID + page, source freshness and contradiction checks; quote/reference only actual passages. A historical case does not override a manufacturer's updated safety bulletin or warning.
4. If a *specific next diagnostic step* is fully supported, answer with no paid live Search. Prefer a reviewed deterministic step/template when appropriate, otherwise low-cost model synthesis with only approved passages. Maintain homeowner safe-observations and technician-only professional diagnostic boundaries. Do not claim "zero API cost" unless genuinely no paid model/embedding call occurred.
5. If symptom mismatched, evidence stale, high hazard, multiple plausible causes, missing model, insufficient detail, or lesson withdrawn, ask one clarifying question or invoke vetted OEM web search as needed. Account for genuine Search query billing and retries. If no source verifies a safe step, state uncertainty; do not fabricate a repair to protect margins. Technician mode should keep troubleshooting when safe rather than generic "call a technician."
6. Record whether the response actually **used** an internal reference, whether a web tool was invoked, actual source IDs and citations, ground truth outcome (when known), paid token/query consumption and safety decision. Log non-sensitive metadata only.

Beware the difference between (a) qualified internal *candidate found* and (b) final correct answer **actually supported** by internal evidence. Attribute cost avoidance only to (b), confirmed by actual provider tool-usage records where available; unknown billing counts are `unpriced`, not $0.

## 4. Economics: no automatic price reduction, no silent quality regressions
- Subscription prices and allowances are owned by the versioned commercial catalog. Learning events, KB-hit ratio, provider price changes and budget optimization can **never** automatically write `plan_name`, `seat_limit`, `monthly_session_allowance`, catalog price or Stripe prices.
- Measure actual variable **cost per completed company-pooled diagnostic CASE**, not merely cost per prompt, and separate expected case costs from actual billed usage. For each case/tenant/month record provider model, input/output (and other billable) tokens, embeddings, OCR/ingestion cost, storage/indexing, *actual paid Search queries* (per respective model rules), retries, timeouts and any external services. Allocate fixed infrastructure and support separately. Reconcile with provider invoices; preserve timestamped cost rate versions.
- Record source-routing metrics: `verified_kb_eligible`, `final_answer_supported_by_internal`, `external_tool_invoked`, `search_query_count`, `verification_or_fallback_reason`, `safety_escalation`, `case_resolution_outcome`, `cost_source`, all keyed by company/case in access-controlled telemetry, redacted. Never log customer statements/model transcript in cost events.
- Dashboard: percentage of finished cases internally resolved *without actual billable web lookup*, externally researched %, successful verified repair %, mean/p50/p95 variable cost per case, variance against provider invoice, stale/wrong-citation rate, approved new lessons, reviewer backlog/denials, tenant-specific vs shared knowledge effectiveness and rolling cohort changes. Show denominator/sample size and "unknown" where not measured.
- Add server-side per-company/per-case spend safeguards, model/search retry ceilings and alerts. Never reduce quality/safety merely to protect budget. If forced to budget-limit a path, retain non-invasive advice, cited existing manuals, useful notes and a transparent status; do not fabricate OEM claims or pretend web search occurred.
- Track net contribution at the **unchanged approved subscription price** minus measured variable consumption, payment fees and allocated storage/support/infra. Rising model efficiency grows margin; it does NOT change customer price or session allowance. Direct fixed per-tier overhead or exceptional support costs may keep absolute net margins below projections even as cost/case falls.

## 5. Minimum executable verification / acceptance gates
Build *behavioral tests against real handler helper functions and a synthetic isolated database* (or explicitly mocked provider with realistic billing metadata), not only `assert.match(regex)`:
1. **Repeat-symptom win:** approved, current, exact model/revision AND matching reported symptom/code, review status + evidence + rights pass -> one complete cited safe next step, **no Search tool call**, cheaper response if safe; same case consumes one company-pooled session even after followups.
2. **Wrong symptom protection:** a correct make/model's unrelated verified lesson cannot block Search, cannot be cited as a fix. Same for mismatched model, old firmware, conflicting revision, unrelated category/code, metadata-only/manual contents with 80+ irrelevant characters, or stale/revoked/unreviewed lesson.
3. **Incomplete evidence:** relevant but partial internal guidance -> use it for one supported observation only, ask clarification or grounded web lookup for next repair; never invent missing parts/measurements.
4. **Safety tests:** ordinary pump no-start does not trigger false homeowner emergency hold; gas/CO, electrical danger, pressure/suction/entrapment and unsafe chemical combinations retain non-bypassable restrictions regardless of KB match, search availability or budget. Technician diagnosis follows professional safety gates, no customer dispatch boilerplate.
5. **Learning admission:** open job or assistant-suggested step never becomes verified fix. Technician-marked resolved with supporting observations can enter review, but cannot publish without company + case consent, evidence, platform review; demo customer reports do not inherit technician verification. Duplicate submissions idempotent.
6. **Cross-tenant and withdrawal:** A cannot retrieve B's private SOP/manual/job; company and individual consent withdrawal excludes pending/approved derived global lessons from retrieval immediately, including stale caches and embeddings; evidence/tenant scope verified on every read.
7. **Cost tracking:** provider Search tool disabled on fully internally supported step; Search enabled/invoked when needed; count actual paid *queries* vs interactions; retry/failure/partial billing correctly attributed; no missing events silently treated as zero cost. Verify rate/version rollover including 2027 prices and real invoice reconciliation.
8. **Commercial separation:** improved internal hit rate leaves **all approved subscription prices, paid add-on prices, seats and company-pooled monthly allowances unchanged**. No cross-company pooling or per-technician quotas. Billing remains disabled until separate production authorization.
9. **Deployment parity:** migration dry-run from clean DB, RLS/access tests, Edge function syntax/tests, full GitHub CI, website and Release Bundle parity, READY UI-only Vercel preview at exact SHA. Record true browser coverage, not inferred. E2E live services require dedicated isolated staging and its own authorization.
